# Data dictionary

Generated by `notebooks/03_preprocessing.ipynb`. Do not edit by hand.

## Sources

| Source | Role | Rows | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| ISIC Archive, clinical close-ups | labelled image-model pool | 6,404 images | CC-0 / CC-BY / CC-BY-NC, per image |
| PAD-UFES-20 | target domain, frozen patient-level train/val/test split | 2,298 images | CC BY 4.0 |

Labels and splits for PAD come from the official Mendeley metadata. The image files come from a public
mirror of the same dataset; all 2,298 filenames match the official ids, and every file was checked against
PAD's own copy inside ISIC (collection 406) by perceptual hash (notebook 03, Cell 5e):

- 100% of mirror files are within pHash distance 4 of an ISIC-406 image, and 87.6% are identical; the rest
  differ only by PNG vs. JPEG compression.
- 2,282 distinct ISIC images are matched; the 16 shared matches are near-identical repeat photographs.
- The malignant flag agrees for 99.7% of images. The 7 disagreements are the same photographs (distance 0–2)
  labelled differently by ISIC, consistently per lesion. They reflect ISIC's re-curation, not a mirror error;
  **the Mendeley labels are kept**.

## `pad_symptom_features.csv`

One row per lesion (`patient_id::lesion_id`), not per photograph: the answers are identical across every
image of a lesion, so one row per photo would weight lesions by how often they were photographed.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `lesion_key` | `patient_id::lesion_id`; the row identifier |
| `patient_id` | for patient-level aggregation (max over lesions) |
| `age` | years, as reported |
| `region` | body site, PAD vocabulary |
| `itch`, `grew`, `hurt`, `changed`, `bleed`, `elevation` | the six questions the app asks; True / False / missing |
| `label` | 1 = MEL, BCC or SCC. ACK counts as 0 |
| `split` | patient-level; `test` is frozen and untouchable |

**Fields deliberately excluded from the features:** `biopsed` (records that a biopsy took place, i.e. the outcome), and the optional fields `gender`, `smoke`, `drink`, `pesticide`, `skin_cancer_history`, `cancer_history`, `has_piped_water`, `has_sewage_system`, `fitspatrick`, `diameter_1`, `diameter_2`. Those are missing mainly for benign cases, so their *absence* predicts the label for administrative rather than medical reasons (missing optional-field count alone: AUC 0.839).

**Missing values are kept as `NaN` in the file.** `grew` and `changed` are unanswered for 20% of development lesions, 87–88% of them malignant, so a missingness indicator would leak the label. The models therefore impute inside the training pipeline (most frequent answer) and never receive an indicator. Imputing the usual answer, "no", makes these mostly malignant lesions look *less* suspicious, so the policy can only lower the baseline, never inflate it. The honest baseline is **AUC 0.860** (notebook 02, variant D).

**Who filled the form:** senior medical students during the visit (Pacheco et al., 2020). App users will answer alone; flipping 10% of answers lowers the baseline's sensitivity at the primary operating point from 0.52 to 0.47 (notebook 02, Cell 8c).

## `aggregation_policy.json`

The frozen rule for turning image scores into the patient score that triage is evaluated on:
`image -> lesion` by **max**, `lesion -> patient` by **max**. A patient's priority is their most suspicious lesion; averaging would dilute it. A patient who submitted several photographs gets several chances at a high score, which raises both sensitivity and false alarms for that patient.

## Baselines the models are measured against (notebook 02)

- `baseline_metadata_val.csv`: questionnaire-only sensitivity, PPV and flag rate on validation patients, 9 scenarios (5/10/15 slots for 100 arrivals × 5/10/20% prevalence). Primary scenario (10 slots, 10% prevalence): sensitivity 0.52, PPV 0.53.
- `val_scores_metadata.csv`: per-patient questionnaire scores on the same 220 validation patients, for a paired comparison with the image and fusion models.

## Transformations

- ISIC images resized so the long side is 512 px, saved as JPEG quality 92, packed into one tar.
- PAD images are stored at their original size under `data/raw/images/pad_original/`.
- All splits use `random_state=42`, are created once and loaded on every later run.
- PAD missing values arrive as `UNK` or an empty string, and are normalised to `NaN` on load.
